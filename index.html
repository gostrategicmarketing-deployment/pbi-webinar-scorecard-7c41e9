How the numbers are built

Webinar ads only. Every count is a person who registered for the weekly webinar through a Meta webinar ad. People who came from organic posts, the past-cohort list or another funnel are left out.

The ad. Website registrations carry the ad in the page link. Meta lead-form registrations carry it in GoHighLevel's copy of the lead form. Each person is credited to the ad on their own registration that week.

Show-up and booked call. GoHighLevel's tags for that webinar (attended; booked call under both tag names used since October), plus any Profit Path booking from the webinar calendar that week that never got a tag.

Call held and purchase. From the GoHighLevel Closer Pipeline. A call counts as held once the booker's deal has moved past scheduled, no-show or reschedule. A purchase is a closed-won deal, dated when it was won and valued at its contract value; a buyer tagged as a Boutique Breakthrough purchase but missing from the pipeline is still counted.

Spend. Meta's spend per ad, hour by hour, from noon Central on the Monday before each webinar to noon Central on webinar day. Lead-form spend is spend in the lead-ad campaigns.

What it cannot see. A booking or purchase made under a different email than the registration cannot be tied to an ad, so these counts are a floor. The newest finished webinar keeps collecting bookings for about a week.

Retired creatives stay in the table for the record with a Retired label. They are switched off on purpose and are never ranked as winners.
